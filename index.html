About me

I am a computer science graduate, with a focus on algorithms, complexity theory, and graph theory. I completed my undergraduate degree from University of Birmingham, where I worked with Dr. Rajesh Chitnis for my final year thesis. After graduation, I am continuing the research with Rajesh's group for this academic year. We are currently working on parameterized algorithms, fine-grained lower bounds and hardness of approximation for problems originating from graph theory and geometry.
